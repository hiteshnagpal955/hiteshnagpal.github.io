PMI Manitoba 2026 Project of the Year Voting Platform

A member-gated digital voting experience built from stakeholder requirements and validated through functional and authentication testing.

Project Snapshot

Organization: PMI Manitoba

Role: Business Data Analyst / Project Contributor

Stakeholders: Project of the Year Chairperson, PMI Manitoba VP, chapter members

Tools: Internal website platform, Google Forms

Focus: Requirements, access workflow, testing, implementation

Business Problem

PMI Manitoba needed an online voting process for the 2026 Project of the Year initiative. A key requirement was that the voting page should be accessible through the chapter website only after a member successfully authenticated through the website login process.

Project Objective

Design and implement a member-gated voting experience within the existing PMI Manitoba website, integrate the voting form, and verify that unauthenticated users were redirected to login while authenticated members could access the voting page.

My Contribution

  • Gathered and documented requirements from the Project of the Year Chairperson and PMI Manitoba VP.
  • Translated requirements into functional requirements, use cases, and user stories.
  • Defined the login-redirection and authenticated-access workflow.
  • Configured the voting page using the organization's internal website tools.
  • Integrated the Google Form used for voting.
  • Tested authenticated and unauthenticated access scenarios.
  • Verified the end-to-end workflow with stakeholders.

Core User Story

As a PMI Manitoba member, I want to access the Project of the Year voting page after successfully logging into my member account so that I can participate in the voting process.

Testing

Functional Validation

Unauthenticated Access

Verified that a visitor attempting to open the voting page while logged out was redirected to the login page.

Authenticated Access

Verified that members could access the voting page after successfully logging into the chapter website.

Voting Flow

Verified that the embedded Google Form loaded correctly and supported the intended voting workflow.

Business Value

The solution created a controlled digital voting experience within PMI Manitoba's existing website and gave authenticated members a straightforward path to participate in the 2026 Project of the Year voting process. The project demonstrated the translation of stakeholder requirements into user flows, system behavior, testing scenarios, and a working implementation.

The website authentication controls access to the voting page. The Google Form is a separate component, so any claim that only eligible members can submit votes should also be supported by the form's own access or eligibility settings.
