PMI Manitoba Membership & Engagement Analytics

A current-state executive dashboard developed to support the Annual General Meeting and strategic-planning discussions.

Project Snapshot

Organization: PMI Manitoba

Purpose: Annual General Meeting & strategic planning

Project type: Analytics & executive reporting

Audience: Chapter leadership and stakeholders

Deliverable: Executive current-state dashboard

Business Problem

PMI Manitoba leadership required a consolidated view of the chapter's current organizational position before the Annual General Meeting and strategic-planning discussions. Membership, certification, industry representation, and communication-channel statistics needed to be brought together into a single management-level view.

Project Objective

Develop an executive-level dashboard that consolidated key chapter metrics and provided leadership with a clear current-state baseline for discussion, planning, and future measurement.

My Contribution

  • Organized the key metrics required for leadership reporting.
  • Consolidated membership, certification, industry, and communication-channel information.
  • Reviewed and structured the information for analysis and presentation.
  • Calculated and presented chapter-level KPIs and distributions.
  • Translated the findings into a concise executive dashboard.
Key Findings

Current-State Snapshot

1,713Active members
1,188Certified members
525Non-certified members
4+Communication channels represented

Business Value

The dashboard transformed multiple organizational metrics into one executive-level view, establishing a current-state baseline across membership, certification, industry representation, and digital reach. This supported AGM discussions and strategic planning by enabling leadership to evaluate the chapter's position and identify areas requiring further attention or future measurement.

Certification counts may overlap where an individual holds more than one credential. The methodology should be explained when discussing credential counts versus unique certified members.
